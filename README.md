# Protein Pantry, Decoded

An outside-in growth teardown of Protein Pantry (Slappin Foods Pvt Ltd), October 2026, by Preetam Priyabrat.

`index.html` is the main story: detailed context (brand, product edge, timeline, category, demand, founders' concerns), the three connected gaps, a fix for each (win the Blinkit trial with stock, search, ads and ratings; one claims register so every surface shows the same numbers; borrowed trust from ratings, Trustified and label readers), everything else I found, and why me, including my own street sale of Protein Pantry.

Every other page is a deep dive from the original teardown: founders, market, competition, product and claims, factory and cold chain, working capital, quick commerce, shelf and channels, brand and ads, voice of customer, trust, diagnosis, playbook, numbers, sources.

## Structure

```
index.html + 15 deep-dive pages
img/          images used by the pages
protein-pantry-similarweb-apr-sep-2026.pdf
```
